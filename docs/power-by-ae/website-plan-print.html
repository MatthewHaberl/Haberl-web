The website plan, in plain terms

What we are building, what it costs, and the steps in order. Nothing here needs you to be technical. The two things I need from you are marked.

What we are building

One website that does four jobs and grows with us without being rebuilt:

  • Public site at powerbyae.co.za. Services, pages the admin person can build herself, and a quote request form.
  • Customer portal. Customers see their quotes, accept them online, see their documents and their solar system's performance.
  • Our office. Quoting, jobs, invoicing, staff hours, monitoring and finance. You and I both log in here, each with our own login.
  • Online store, later. Retail first, wholesale accounts when we open them.
